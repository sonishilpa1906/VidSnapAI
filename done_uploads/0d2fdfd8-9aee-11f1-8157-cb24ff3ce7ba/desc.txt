The professional summary is a brief section at the beginning of a resume that provides an overview of a candidate’s professional experience and skills. This section should typically include information about your most notable achievements, skills, and qualifications, as well as a summary of your career goals. It’s important to include a professional summary in your resume because it’s the first thing potential employers will look for when reviewing your application. A well-written professional summary can grab their attention and give them a quick overview of your strengths and qualifications. It is one of the best opportunities to stand out from other applicants.
